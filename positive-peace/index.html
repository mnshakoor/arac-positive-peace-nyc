Framework

Positive Peace, applied to the neighborhoods of New York

The Institute for Economics & Peace (IEP) defines Positive Peace as the attitudes, institutions and structures that create and sustain peaceful societies, measured through eight interdependent Pillars. ARAC's Positive Peace work is led by M. Nuri Shakoor, an IEP Positive Peace Ambassador. This site applies the framework at the scale of a city: each story is tagged with the Pillars it illustrates.

A systems view

IEP treats the Pillars as a system: they reinforce one another, and progress in one tends to depend on the others. A single event rarely moves a Pillar on its own. Our coverage records where community activity connects to each Pillar; it does not claim to measure peace.
