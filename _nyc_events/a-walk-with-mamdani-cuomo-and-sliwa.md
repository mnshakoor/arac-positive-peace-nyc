## Introduction

In an era often defined by ideological divides and political polarization, finding common ground can feel like a rare occurrence. Yet, true community resilience and peacebuilding are not built on consensus alone—they are fostered when individuals from across different spectrums share space, engage in open dialogue, and celebrate the shared human experience.

During the August 2025 Dominican Day Parade in New York City, captured in the video "A Walk With Mamdani, Cuomo, and Sliwa", we observed a vivid illustration of civic engagement in action. As a Positive Peace Ambassador, observing these dynamic interactions among key political and community figures—including three candidates in the 2025 New York City mayoral race, then New York State Assembly Member Zohran Mamdani, former New York Governor Andrew Cuomo, and Guardian Angels founder Curtis Sliwa—provides valuable insights into the mechanics of social cohesion.

## Highlights from the Walk

The short video documents a vibrant street parade filled with music, waving Dominican flags, and community celebration:

- **Celebrating Community with Zohran Mamdani:** The video opens with then Assembly Member Zohran Mamdani walking alongside parade participants, waving flags, and joining in community chants. This active presence highlights the importance of political representatives directly connecting with cultural events that define the city's rich diaspora.
- **Dialogue on Due Process with Andrew Cuomo:** Former Governor Andrew Cuomo speaks on the street regarding federal government policies and legal rights. In his exchange, Cuomo emphasizes the fundamental importance of legal process and standing up for basic rights, stating:

  > "You cannot deport until you have the process of the law... someone has to stand up and stand up for basic legal rights and due process."

- **Street-Level Engagement with Curtis Sliwa:** Curtis Sliwa, wearing his signature red beret, is seen greeting parade-goers, shaking hands with local workers, and interacting directly with children and families along the route. His presence emphasizes civic visibility and community protection networks.

## Analysis: Positive Peace in Action

At ARAC International, our approach to peacebuilding is guided by the Positive Peace Framework, developed by the Institute for Economics & Peace (IEP). Positive Peace is defined not merely as the absence of violence (Negative Peace), but as the presence of the attitudes, institutions, and structures that create and sustain peaceful societies.

Watching these distinct political figures navigate the same public space offers several key takeaways aligned with the Pillars of Positive Peace:

### 1. Well-Functioning Government & Acceptance of the Rights of Others

Due process and constitutional rights form the bedrock of institutional trust. As highlighted during the dialogue with Andrew Cuomo, ensuring legal protections for all residents—regardless of status—is essential to maintaining societal stability and justice. When institutions honor due process, community trust in public systems increases.

### 2. High Levels of Human Capital & Good Relations with Neighbors

Cultural parades and civic celebrations serve as vital platforms for building social capital. By participating in cultural milestones like Dominican Heritage celebrations, public figures signal respect and inclusion toward diverse communities. Strong neighborly relations foster local resilience and mutual understanding.

### 3. Open Dialogue and Public Visibility

Civic spaces thrive when leaders walk among the people rather than remain isolated behind closed doors. Whether answering questions on the street or shaking hands with parade attendees, visible engagement reduces barriers between the public and leadership.

## Conclusion

Building peace within complex urban environments requires continuous effort, respectful engagement, and active community participation. While political figures may hold contrasting perspectives on governance, security, and policy, shared civic events remind us of our collective humanity and common goals.

By documenting and analyzing these moments, ARAC International reaffirms its commitment to promoting human rights, rule of law, and positive peace across communities worldwide.
