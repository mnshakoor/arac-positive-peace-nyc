Help build a more resilient community with THINK

THINK is a free media literacy initiative from ARAC International's advocacy programs. Its guides and toolkits help residents examine information before acting on it, which builds the community resilience that Positive Peace depends on. Positive Peace is a core foundation for human rights and democracy.
