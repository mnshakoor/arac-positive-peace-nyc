About

A community newsroom for Positive Peace

ARAC Community NYC is the community media site of ARAC International Inc. Volunteer journalists, photographers and educators document the cultural life of New York City, connect it to the Pillars of Positive Peace, and promote media and digital literacy through THINK.

Editorial Standards
  • Every photograph is credited to the volunteer who made it.
  • Factual claims link to their sources. Coverage separates what was observed from what is interpreted.
  • Pillar tags describe how an event connects to Positive Peace. They are not a measurement of peace.
  • Event coverage does not imply endorsement by, or affiliation with, the City of New York or any City agency.
