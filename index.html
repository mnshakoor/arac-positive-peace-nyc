Our Mandate

Three commitments to New York City

01

Positive Peace in the City

We promote and document cultural awareness events sponsored by the City of New York that reflect the eight Pillars of Positive Peace, as part of ARAC's Positive Peace mandate, led by M. Nuri Shakoor as an IEP Positive Peace Ambassador.

The eight Pillars →
02

Media and Digital Literacy for All

Through THINK services and toolkits we build critical thinking, digital and AI literacy, and advocate for community tools and services so that no community is left behind.

Visit THINK →
03

Diaspora and Community Understanding

We cover events within New York's international communities to understand the social, political and economic issues they face, and highlight gatherings that promote unity and goodwill.

Framework

Public Service Initiative
THINK
think.arac-international.org

Media and digital literacy, free and open to every community

THINK is ARAC International's public service education initiative for media literacy, digital literacy, critical thinking and AI literacy. Our community coverage and THINK share one aim: residents who can examine a claim before acting on it.

We do not tell you what to think. We help you think about information.
Visit THINK Get Updates
Media LiteracyReading a story for what it establishes, what it implies and what it leaves out.
Digital LiteracyUsing platforms and online services safely and with confidence.
Critical ThinkingTesting a claim, weighing evidence and recognizing a weak argument.
AI LiteracyWhat generative tools do well, where they fail, and how synthetic content spreads.
